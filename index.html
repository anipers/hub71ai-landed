A company lands when its people land. The founder or HR sees the company track and only the work steps of each employee: visa, medical, Emirates ID, payroll bank account. Housing, family and social life stay private. Inviting someone starts their own journey in the app.

Team

Work steps only. Each person can hide any of them; personal documents never leave their phone.

Landed brings no new technology. Everything a newcomer needs already exists: ACCESSADGM, ICP, TAMM, UAE PASS, SEHA, banks, schools, ILOE, Hub71. The problem is that it is scattered, and people find it too late or in the wrong order. Landed puts it into one personal plan and opens each tool at the right moment.

Every stage, linked to what already exists

Landed never files a visa, registers a lease or holds documents. Those stay with their owners.
